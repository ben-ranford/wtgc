# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Project spaces should be practical, respectful, and focused on improving wtgc.
Examples of expected behavior include:

- Being clear and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Assuming good intent while still asking for evidence and tests.
- Respecting maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and release readiness.
- Reporting security-sensitive problems through the private security process.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, deliberate
disruption, doxxing, or publishing private vulnerability details before a fix is
available.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or
other contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations can
lead to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be
reviewed privately and handled according to the severity and context of the
incident.
